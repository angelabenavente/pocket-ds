# Typography

Typography styles provide consistent hierarchy and rhythm across interface content.

Heading is used for page and section titles. Body is used for regular text. Button is used for clickable nodes, including tabs.

<TypographyTable rows={typographyStyles} />

The Sass presets are `heading-m`, `heading-s`, `body-m`, `body-s`, `button-m`, and `button-s`. CircularXX is used for headings and Inter for body and button styles; both include system fallbacks until font delivery is added.
